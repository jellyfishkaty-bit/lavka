## Атлас предметов — prompt

Create a production 2D pixel-art game sprite ATLAS, using attached image ONLY as artistic style/reference, NOT as an image to preserve or flatten. EXACTLY 4 columns x 3 rows of separate isolated sprites on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Same orthographic front-view dollhouse viewpoint and same pixel scale, crisp deliberate pixel clusters, detailed 1990s adventure-game pixel art, warm walnut wood / amber copper / parchment / burgundy plum / desaturated teal. Not watercolor, not 3D, not blurry AI painted 'pixel effect'. Each item centered within its own equally sized cell, at least 15% transparent padding to every cell edge, items NEVER cross cell boundaries, no shared floor, no contact shadow outside item, no captions, no text, no cell borders. Wide landscape atlas ideally 1536x1152. All objects full silhouette with alpha, suitable for extraction into individual movable game objects. Row 1 left to right: 1) plush burgundy armchair with cream and teal knitted throw draped over arm; 2) ornate walnut work desk with two drawers, gold handles, burgundy/cream/teal chevron knitted stole hanging from desk to front, yarn and knitting needles, NO lamp NO mug; 3) tall walnut bookcase filled with old burgundy and brown books and cream/teal/plum yarn balls, small model sailboat on top; 4) wall-mounted brass rounded-top letter mailbox with cream envelopes and red wax seal. Row 2: 1) closed antique wooden treasure chest with copper straps, no glowing magic; 2) woven wicker basket full of teal, cream, burgundy yarn and two wooden knitting needles; 3) curled sleeping tabby and white cat, visible face and tail; 4) richly woven Persian rug, teal/plum/cream floral geometry, front-view foreshortened shallow horizontal rug. Row 3: 1) antique curved brass desk lamp with glowing amber scalloped fabric shade and brass base, self-contained object, no surrounding glow; 2) small ivory ceramic mug with delicate dark floral pattern, no steam; 3) hanging strand of dark-green ivy with warm olive highlights; 4) large carved walnut window FRAME, front view, slightly arched top, vertical central mullion and two horizontal bars, empty genuinely transparent panes and exterior, no glass paint, no harbor inside. Preserve consistency with reference in cozy detail, texture, lighting and colors. These are game assets, not an interface screenshot.

## Гавань — prompt

Draw a NEW single game background using attached image solely as style/reference. Detailed authentic crisp 2D PIXEL ART background showing a sleepy old seaside harbor through a workshop window, frontal view at dusk. Tall portrait composition approximately 4:5. Small coastal houses on left and right with steep roofs, slate blue roofs, burgundy details, warm amber glowing windows, stone quay, old lamps, one modest sailing boat moored in middle lower part, distant lighthouse and calm harbor with tiny gold reflections. Cool navy / steel-blue / muted teal water, cloudy dusk sky. Cozy quietly melancholic atmosphere matching attached knitting-shop concept. Draw only the OUTDOOR harbor view: NO interior, NO window frame, NO armchair, NO desk, NO people, NO text, NO signage, NO UI. Rain will be animated by the game, so do not bake large rain streaks into the image. Hand-crafted consistent visible pixel clusters, restrained 40-color style, precise stepped edges, not 3D, not photograph, not painterly, no blurred antialiasing. This will be a separate replaceable layer behind a carved wood window frame.
